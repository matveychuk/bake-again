  2 tbsp sunflower oil, 160 g Schär Mix B, 65 g unsweetened cocoa, 8 g baking powder.
- The author explicitly clarified that condensed milk was **70 g**, not the 100 g
  in the trial card. Cocoa was **65 g**, not the proposed 35 g.

- Cream: **700 g combined sour cream and Greek yogurt**, 70 g sugar, 1 spoon lemon

- 150 g prunes, with some reserved for decoration; 30 g walnuts or pecans for topping.
- Tin: **24 cm**, explicitly corrected by the author after the initial recipe update.
- Oven: **200°C, 30 minutes with foil loosely laid over the top, then 20 minutes

  measurement for the cake is not silently applied to the separately maintained glaze.

## Final method clarification

The cooled cake is cut horizontally into two layers. About 1 cm is trimmed from the full circumference of each layer; these edge trimmings are cut into small pieces and reserved. Both layers receive sour cream and prune filling, and the remaining cream coats the trimmings for the mound. No third cake layer is used. Eggs are used straight from the refrigerator; the room-temperature requirement and weighing advice were removed. The public evidence remains Tested with Schär Mix B and a 24 cm tin, without author/date or repeated-bake claims.
