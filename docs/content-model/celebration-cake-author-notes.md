# Celebration sponge cake — author record, 2026-09-25

Working title: Святковий бісквітний торт із фруктами.

Confirmed by the author: 8 eggs, 225 g sugar (150 g whites + 75 g yolks), 225 g Schär Mix B, 24–25 g corn starch, 10–12 g tapioca starch, salt and vanilla; round 24 cm tin; crossed foil lining; lay foil loosely over the top after 30 minutes. This formula is independent of mechanically scaling the existing five-egg sponge. Once the centre tests dry, switch off the oven, open the door slightly and leave 10–15 minutes; cool fully and cut into three layers.

Filling: 500 g sour cream and 3 tablespoons sugar for BOTH filling layers together, optional tablespoon lemon juice. Stir with a spoon or whip with a hand/stand mixer. No 1–1.5 cm thickness promise: the confirmed filling amount is distributed equally. Optional fruit can go into both layers and on top.
