# Banana bread working image candidates

These images are working candidates, not publication-approved recipe media. They were
generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for the Schär Mix B Bread version of the recipe.

## Candidates

- `ingredients-and-tin-v1.png` — ingredients and prepared loaf tin; supports step 1.
- `batter-in-tin-v1.png` — batter filling the parchment-lined tin; supports step 5.
- `doneness-skewer-v1.png` — baked loaf and skewer with moist crumbs; supports step 6.
- `cooled-sliced-loaf-v1.png` — cooled loaf and visible crumb; supports step 7 or the hero.

## Shared direction

- Warm-neutral stone, natural linen and simple matte ceramics.
- Food is visually dominant; no people, packaging, labels, text, logos or watermarks.
- The images are instructional rather than rustic or decorative.
