## Reference pack

- `kitchen-master-4x3.png`: primary three-quarter prep view with visible upper cabinets,
  striped linen towel, pale beech board and cream storage jar.
- `kitchen-prep-closeup-4x3.png`: higher, closer prep-counter view for batter texture
  and small actions; matching materials, props and left-front daylight.
- `kitchen-stovetop-master-4x3.png`, `kitchen-stovetop-overhead-4x3.png` and
  `kitchen-stovetop-turning-closeup-4x3.png`: rejected first stovetop direction,
  preserved as selection history. It incorrectly placed an implausibly control-free
  pale cooktop in the established preparation-counter location.

## Stovetop identity — S2-B selected

- The stove belongs to a separate, adjacent cooking wall in the same L-shaped kitchen;
  it must not replace the established preparation counter with its board and jar.
- Use a physically credible black induction surface with integrated touch controls,
  a built-in oven below and a concealed extractor above.
- Keep small signs of use and restrained decorative objects, while leaving the active
  burner clear for a 24 cm pan, handle, spatula and hand.
- Use the S2-B cooking wall as the stable background for frying photographs. Derive
  closer or higher angles from this master without changing its appliance and prop
  identity.

## Shared direction

- Use case: `photorealistic-natural`.
- Intended use: stable 4:3 background reference for Bake Again recipe-step photography.
- Keep a broad, uncluttered prep surface with crop-safe margins.
- Use soft directional daylight from the left and restrained, realistic shadows.
- Keep whites colour-accurate and the palette warm cream, pale stone and soft greige.
- Use believable matte materials with subtle natural texture.
- Keep the scene practical and editorial rather than decorative or showroom-like.
- Do not include food, bowls, people, hands, utensils, appliances, text, logos or
  watermarks in the master reference.
- Avoid patterned tiles, rustic or farmhouse styling, wellness cues, plants, table
  linens, staged props, artificial gloss, strong yellow casts and heavy sepia.

## Selection workflow

1. Use `kitchen-master-4x3.png` as the visual-world master for wider prep photographs.
2. Use `kitchen-prep-closeup-4x3.png` for tight preparation and texture photographs.
3. Preserve all candidate files and the empty master as selection history.
4. Use `kitchen-stovetop-master-v2-4x3.png` for stovetop photographs; preserve the same
   black induction hob and controls, oven, rail, potholder, wooden spoon, countertop
   objects and left-side daylight in every derived angle.
5. Edit recipe photographs one at a time using the source photograph as the edit target
   and the relevant kitchen reference as the supporting style/background input.
6. Repeat subject invariants in every edit: preserve the real food state, utensils,
   cookware, geometry and instructional meaning; change only the environment, framing
   and lighting requested for the edit.
7. Save approved recipe media as versioned assets; never overwrite originals.
